# Daily Closing Checklist

Complete this checklist at the end of every shift before handing off or locking up.

---

## Check-out wrap-up

- [ ] Confirm all expected check-outs for today have been processed in StayDesk.
- [ ] Any guest who has not checked out by 12:00 PM — contact manager.
- [ ] Confirm payment was collected for all check-outs (no open folios remaining).

---

## Arrivals wrap-up

- [ ] Confirm all expected check-ins for today have been processed.
- [ ] Any no-show reservations — flag to manager; do not cancel without approval.
- [ ] Verify all checked-in guests have a room key.

---

## End-of-day system review

- [ ] Review tomorrow's arrivals on the calendar — note count and any special requests.
- [ ] Confirm tomorrow's arrival rooms are assigned and cleaned or scheduled for cleaning.
- [ ] Check for any reservations with missing contact information and note for follow-up.

---

## Cash handling (if applicable)

- [ ] Reconcile any cash payments taken during the shift.
- [ ] Complete the cash log and secure the drawer per property policy.

---

## Handoff to overnight / next shift

- [ ] Leave written notes for any unresolved issues, pending questions, or guest needs.
- [ ] Note any rooms that are out of service or have maintenance issues.
- [ ] Log out of StayDesk before leaving the desk unattended.
