## System issues

If StayDesk is unresponsive or you cannot process a check-in or payment:

1. Do not turn the guest away — offer them a seat and call the manager.
2. Record the guest's name, room number, and arrival time on paper.
3. Notify the manager immediately.
4. The manager will contact technical support if the outage is system-wide.

> **Keep a paper log book at the front desk** for situations where the system is unavailable.

---

## Payment system issues

If payments fail to process:

1. Note the error message shown on screen (photograph it if possible).
2. Do not attempt to run the card more than twice — multiple failed attempts can flag the guest's card.
3. Ask the guest for an alternate form of payment.
4. Contact the manager if the issue persists.

---

## Terminal / bridge down

If the card terminal won't process a charge:

1. Check the check-in screen — if it shows "Terminal isn't responding," that's the bridge, not the terminal itself.
2. Confirm the front-desk laptop is powered on and connected to the network.
3. Restart the `StayDeskBridge` Windows service (open **Services**, find `StayDeskBridge`, click Restart).
4. Still down? Use **Enter Card Manually** to take the card another way, or switch to record-only mode if instructed by the manager, and notify the manager either way.

## Guest emergencies

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Medical emergency | Call 911, then notify manager |
| Fire | Activate alarm, evacuate, call 911, then manager |
| Guest locked out of room | Verify identity against reservation before issuing replacement key |
| Disturbance / threatening guest | Call 911 if safety is at risk; do not confront alone |

---

## Data / security incidents

If you suspect unauthorized access to guest data or the StayDesk system:

1. Do not attempt to investigate yourself.
2. Note the time and what you observed.
3. Contact the manager immediately.
4. Do not discuss the incident with other guests or staff until instructed by management.
