What's included

Everything a small property needs. Nothing it doesn't.

🛏

Guest management

Rooms, reservations, check-in and check-out, and per-stay folios with itemized charges.

💳

Payments via Stripe

Collect payment at checkout or hold a card on file. Stripe handles the complexity.

👥

Payroll via Gusto

Track employee hours and run payroll through Gusto's API. No spreadsheets required.

📊

Simple dashboard

Today's arrivals, departures, and occupancy at a glance. The info you actually need.
